Getting Here

Commercial Heart of Sango Otta

PointPlux Hotels & Suites is located at the commercial business area of Sango Otta, some buildings away from the popular Abeokuta/Lagos Express Road on Joju Road. We are surrounded by over ten commercial banks, and close to the tourism centres and most popular places in Nigeria listed below.

  • 120 minute drive to the popular ancient Olumo Rock
  • 220 minute drive to MMM Airport, Ikeja
  • 315 minutes drive to the Faith Tabernacle of Living Faith Church, Bishop David Oyedepo — the second largest church auditorium in the world
  • 415 minute drive to the newly ultra-modern palace of the Olota of Otta
  • 510 minute drive to Covenant University and Bells University
